# J713 capped speaker protection

`conf/apple/j713.conf` is the speaker-protection profile for the MacBook Air 13-inch (M4), J713. It drives the
four internal speakers (two woofers, two tweeters) at 48 kHz with the Aurora audio driver (AOP speaker
back-end, four TAS2764 amps). Like the J813 and J815 profiles it targets comfortable everyday playback, not
full macOS loudness: the digital ceilings are -24 dB for the woofers and -32 dB for the tweeters.

The kernel speaker interlock keeps the amps at the safe level until this daemon holds the speaker lease and
relocks them when it goes away.

## Speakers and feedback

| Speaker | I2C | I/V sense slots | Feedback channels (I, V) | Speaker PCM channel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Left Woofer | 0x38 | 0 / 2 | 0, 1 | 0 |
| Right Woofer | 0x3b | 4 / 6 | 2, 3 | 1 |
| Left Tweeter | 0x39 | 8 / 10 | 4, 5 | 2 |
| Right Tweeter | 0x3c | 12 / 14 | 6, 7 | 3 |

The feedback PCM has 12 S16 channels; 8-11 carry no speaker. Each amp sends current first, then voltage.
The speaker PCM has six channels; 4 and 5 drive nothing.

## Where the values come from

- **Thermal and electrical constants: the J813's.** macOS 27.2 ships the same speaker-protection tuning for
  the J713 and the J813 (the speaker strip, its protection preset and flow data are identical apart from the
  acoustic-id names), so the constants and limits of `j813.conf` carry over unchanged: an 80 °C model limit
  with 5 °C headroom (Apple: 140 °C / 10 °C) and the -24/-32 dB ceilings.
- **`z_nominal` kept at 2.8 Ω.** The measured woofer minimum is 3.8-4.0 Ω and the tweeters' 3.3 Ω. The daemon
  only uses `z_nominal` for the worst-case peak power behind the maximum gain reduction, so a value below the
  measured one is the conservative side.
- **Feedback layout and timing: measured** (below). Left Woofer's current lags its voltage by one sample at
  48 kHz, the mirror of the J813's left woofer, so its section sets `voltage_delay_samples = 1` with
  `voltage_delay_rate = 48000`. The daemon pairs each current sample with the previous voltage sample; the
  first sample after a capture reset is charged at the full-scale bound, as for `current_delay_samples`. A
  speaker may delay one side only; a declaration of both, of more than one sample, or without its rate is
  refused before any mixer control opens.

The profile contains no Apple DSP code or tuning bundle.

## Hardware qualification

One J713 (macOS 27.2 firmware, Aurora Linux 7.3.0-bj713b2+), this profile, the daemon with
`voltage_delay_samples`, started with `-v --sense-capture`.

- **Startup and ceilings.** Four speakers found; `Speaker Volume` read back 153 (-24 dB) on both woofers and
  137 (-32 dB) on both tweeters; the daemon owns `Speaker Volume Unlock`; the feedback PCM runs at 48 kHz.
- **Silence.** A 10 s all-zero stream: live noise on feedback channels 0-7 (|x| up to ~250 counts), channels
  8-11 zero, power 0 W.
- **Mapping.** A 1 kHz burst at -20 dBFS on each speaker channel in turn: each answered on its own feedback
  pair only (the others stayed at the ~3.4 mV floor), positive power.
- **Low-level electrical sweep** (`testing/make_j815_sweep.py` levels: woofers 80 Hz-10 kHz at -12 dBFS,
  tweeters 400 Hz-16 kHz at -18 dBFS, 1.6 s each, under the ceilings), checked with
  `testing/verify_j815_sweep.py`:

  | Speaker | Crosstalk above noise | Order | Lag (current vs voltage) | R_min (Ω) | Resonance peak |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Left Woofer | 0.0 % | I, V | +1 sample | 3.82 | 4.48 Ω @ 535 Hz |
  | Right Woofer | 0.0 % | I, V | 0 | 4.00 | 4.66 Ω @ 536 Hz |
  | Left Tweeter | 0.0 % | I, V | 0 | 3.30 | 3.65 Ω @ 2.27 kHz |
  | Right Tweeter | 0.0 % | I, V | 0 | 3.31 | 3.72 Ω @ 2.34 kHz |

  With the voltage delay the daemon's per-period power matched an independent replay of the capture within
  1e-8 W on every speaker (117 periods each).
- **Listening.** Pink-noise bursts per speaker and a 20 s synthetic music clip (120 Hz high-pass to the
  woofers, 2.8 kHz to the tweeters) at up to -1 dBFS peak: no gain reduction, amp fault status 0, no daemon
  restart, model coil temperature 50.1 °C, woofer average 1.0-1.4 mW (peak 6 mW).
